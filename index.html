About

Painting is a slower kind of conversation.

I work mostly in oil and cold-pressed watercolour, drawn to the moment before a scene resolves into detail — wet light on a coal-town roof, a room remembered more than seen, the pause before rain.

Every piece begins as a small charcoal thumbnail and is built slowly, in thin layers, over several sittings. Replace this paragraph and the portrait panel with your own story and photo whenever you're ready.

— Ruchika Kumari

Featured — Coal & Marigold

Oil on linen, 90 × 120 cm

Named for the coal dust and marigold garlands that share every Jharkhand morning market — this piece took four sittings and one ruined sleeve.
